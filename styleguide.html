Rules

  • Red is for one primary action, the selected animal, and a repeated ancestor. Nothing else.
  • Status never relies on colour alone — every band carries its word.
  • Every figure is tabular, so two numbers can be compared down a column.
  • Pedigree lines are square-cornered. No curve, no rounded join, no terminal dot.
  • Corners are 4px on controls and 6px on cards. No shadows, no gradients, no texture.
  • Body text starts at 16px and goes up, never down.
